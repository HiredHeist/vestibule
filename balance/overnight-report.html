The headline: this is not a game that's too easy. It's a game with no middle. Nearly half of all runs die to the first boss. Almost everything that survives the first three fights goes on to win. Only 10 of 107 runs ended anywhere between Circle 2 and Circle 8.

Where runs end

The difficulty curve is bimodal — a wall at the start, then near-open road. A healthy roguelite loses runs across the whole arc.

Survival funnel

Attrition happens almost entirely in fights 0–3. From fight 3 to fight 26 the game sheds only 11 of 43 runs.

Win rate, conditional on getting there

Reaching fight 3 more than doubles your odds versus starting a run. After that the number barely moves — the run is effectively decided.

Band ATK growth — winners vs runs that died at fight 0

Median band ATK by fight. The two groups start nearly level (17 vs 15); by fight 2 the winners are 44% ahead and compounding. Runs that die at fight 0 bottom out at a median band ATK of 4.

Chain-rate measurement note

The raw ledger contains 15,756 chain_confirmed rows, which is a counting artifact — the detector re-emitted the whole log history after every fight boundary. Deduplicated it is 918 (0.29/strike); the bot's independent inference says 0.21/strike, and the simulator says 0.26/strike. Three methods agree that chains fire roughly once every four to five strikes. The raw figure is the bug I had already reported and has since been fixed.
