# VESTIBULE — 50 Card Ideas for Deck Rethink

**Problem:** Ember cards dominate because they enable longer combos. Playing "generate embers → spend embers" every turn is boring. Cards need to reward DIFFERENT playstyles.

**The ember starvation problem:** Running dry on strike 3 feels TERRIBLE. Ember generation HAS to exist — but at what cost?

**Current ember cards have no real cost:**
- Tapped Out: +5 embers for 0 cost. Pure upside.
- Power Tap: +1 ember for 1 cost = net zero. No downside.
- Static Charge: efficient generation with no tension.

**Better ember economy — generation with MEANINGFUL costs:**
- HP sacrifice (lose max ember or member HP)
- Corruption tax (+3-5% corruption per ember card)
- Card disadvantage (discard to gain)
- Tempo loss (gain embers NEXT strike, not this one)
- Diminishing returns (first ember card = full, second = half)

**The goal:** Ember generation should feel like a DECISION not an auto-include.
